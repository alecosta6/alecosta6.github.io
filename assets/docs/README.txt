# Guide to Lecture Notes, Research Papers & CV

Place your downloadable lecture notes, research pre-prints, and Curriculum Vitae in this directory (`assets/docs/`).

### Suggested File Names matching `index.html`:

1. **Curriculum Vitae**:
   - `CV_Alessio_Costa.pdf` (linked in the Hero and CV sections)

2. **Course Notes & Other Material**:
   - `numerical_analysis_notes.pdf` (Numerical Analysis & Scientific Computing)
   - `complex_analysis_notes.pdf` (Complex Analysis & Hilbert Spaces)
   - `optimization_notes.pdf` (Operations Research & Convex Optimization)
   - `rational_mechanics_notes.pdf` (Analytical Mechanics & Variational Principles)
   - `probability_notes.pdf` (Probability & Mathematical Statistics)

3. **Publications & Working Papers**:
   - Save your thesis or preprint PDFs here (e.g. `thesis_alessio_costa.pdf`, `preprint_pde_solver.pdf`)

---

### How to add a new course note / paper:
1. Copy your compiled PDF into this folder (`assets/docs/my_new_file.pdf`).
2. Open `index.html` and duplicate a `<div class="note-card">` or `<div class="pub-card">` block.
3. Update the title, description, and LaTeX formula inside `$$ ... $$`.
